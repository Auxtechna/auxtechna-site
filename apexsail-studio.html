Studio: your social media, drafted and checked

Studio finds things worth talking about, writes the posts in your voice and under your rules, checks them, and queues them. A person approves each one before anything goes out.

Finds the storyPulls from what you have actually done, or from text you paste in.
Drafts for each channelOne draft per network, written with Claude under your house rules and the claims you have said you can make.
Checks before a person sees itBanned phrases, claims, link domains and channel length limits are checked automatically, then a second review pass flags anything doubtful.
A person approves, then it goes outEdit, approve, schedule or publish. One post at a time, checked again at the moment it is sent.

Nothing posts without a person

There is no bulk schedule and no autopilot. The text that is approved is the text that is checked, and the text that is checked is the text that goes out.

  • Your rules live in a folder you can read and change
  • Every send is single-use, so a double click cannot post twice
  • Connects to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok through Zernio, so Studio never holds your network tokens
📚

One Studio per business

Your guidelines, products and channels sit in a content pack of your own. Same app, your voice.

🛡

Your claims register

Posts may only make claims you have approved. Anything marked do-not-claim is caught before it reaches a reviewer.

📅

Schedule from the story

Approved posts sit under the story they came from, ready to schedule, publish now or post by hand.
